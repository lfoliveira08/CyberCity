## Lessons learned
| Issue | Resolution | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| VM 100 froze with `running (io-error)`, black console, no guest agent. Real cause: the `local-lvm` thin pool (68.5 GiB, VG fully allocated, over-provisioned with about 115 GiB of virtual disks) hit 100% while a teammate cloned a VM; QEMU pauses a guest on the first failed write and stays paused after space returns. | `qm resume 100`. Prevent: move VM 100's disk to `Big_Storage`, clone and back up only to `Big_Storage`, trim and prune, keep pool `Data%` under about 80%. | VMs 100 and 101 on `local-lvm`; any clone, backup or snapshot on `pve`. Verified on VM 100 only; VM 101 was not paused but shares the pool. |
