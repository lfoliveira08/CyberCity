## Result

- **What the traffic shows:** the full recon on the wire — the ARP host-discovery sweep, the SYN probes to `:502`/`:8080`, and the Modbus `modbus-discover` exchanges (the `ILLEGAL FUNCTION` exception replies from the OpenPLC containers). Open with display filters `arp`, then `mbtcp`.
- **Inventory confirmed (verified by scan, 2026-11-10):**
  - 8 OpenPLC containers up — BSH `.111–.114`, FH `.121–.124`; **all 8 expose `:8080`** (web UI runs regardless of PLC state).
  - `:502` is open on a container only while its **PLC is running**. On Nov 9 the FH containers (`.121–.124`) had `:502` **closed** (PLCs stopped); on Nov 10 they were **open** (PLCs running) — so FH PLC run-state is transient.
  - `modbus-discover` → the containers answered `sid 0x1: ILLEGAL FUNCTION` = confirmed live Modbus servers that don't implement the device-ID function (normal for OpenPLC). ESP32s **time out** (ModbusIP doesn't answer fingerprinting) — identify them by Espressif MAC instead. OpenPLC answered unit ID 1 though configured SID 0, i.e. it does not enforce the unit ID.
- **What changed on the system:** nothing — recon is read-only (scan + capture). No writes.
- **Was it detected, and by what:** not monitored for this run (no IDS; the point here is to record the attacker's view).
