04 Before You Power On

Two separate relay boards now, as of Sep 30, 2026. They are not guaranteed to share a polarity — verify each one independently with the Phase A bench-test sketches before trusting either.
The fan relay's earlier active-HIGH finding does not carry over. That result was on the original 2-channel module, which has been replaced. Re-run Step 1 (fan alone) on the new 1-channel board starting from the firmware's default RELAY_FAN_ACTIVE_LOW = true.
Each LED gets its own resistor. Never wire all 3 LEDs off one shared resistor — their forward voltages aren't identical, and current won't split evenly.
Buttons need no external resistor. Firmware uses INPUT_PULLUP — wire each button straight from its GPIO pin to GND.
Keep the fan on its own rail if it draws real current. A small DC motor + blade is usually fine off the ESP32's 5V pin for a bench test; anything bigger should get a separate supply sharing only GND with the ESP32.
